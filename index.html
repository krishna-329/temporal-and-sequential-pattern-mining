Why PrefixSpan Excels

PrefixSpan eliminates the combinatorial candidate explosion of Apriori-based algorithms by recursively projecting suffixes. Only items present in projected databases are explored, yielding up to 10x-50x speedups on dense datasets.

Why SPADE Excels with Temporal Gaps

SPADE transforms the horizontal sequence database into vertical ID-lists $(SID, EID)$. Temporal joins compute frequent sequences through fast set intersections without re-scanning the disk.

System Architecture & Methodology

1. Sequential Pattern Mining (SPM) Engine

Temporal sequence mining discovers frequent sub-sequences $\langle s_1, s_2, \dots, s_k \rangle$ where event orders and time-gap constraints $\Delta t \le t_{\max}$ are preserved:

  • PrefixSpan: Recursively projects suffix databases without candidate generation.
  • SPADE: Uses vertical ID-lists $(SID, EID)$ and temporal joins for high throughput.
  • Temporal Rules: Computes $conf(X \implies Y) = \frac{supp(X \cup Y)}{supp(X)}$ with lag distribution.

2. Privacy-Preserving DP-FedGNN

Identifies cross-silo social & interaction communities across decentralized nodes (hospitals, banks, city nodes):

  • Local GNN: Graph Convolutional message passing $H^{(l+1)} = \sigma(\tilde{D}^{-\frac{1}{2}} \tilde{A} \tilde{D}^{-\frac{1}{2}} H^{(l)} W^{(l)})$.
  • Differential Privacy: Clips gradients $\|g\|_2 \le C$ and injects calibrated Gaussian noise $\mathcal{N}(0, \sigma^2 C^2)$.
  • FedAvg Aggregator: Central coordination without raw data disclosure.
